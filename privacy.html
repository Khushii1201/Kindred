Privacy

Saved trips, profile places, and SOS contacts stay in this browser.

Map services

Place search text goes to Nominatim. Route coordinates go to Valhalla. Route context queries go to Overpass. Map tiles come directly from OpenStreetMap standard raster tiles. Opening a Google Maps link sends selected coordinates to Google.

SOS

SOS asks for your location and prepares a message for the contact saved in this browser. You must tap Open Messages, review it, then tap Send. Kindred does not track you in the background.

Limits

There is no verified live safety feed. Map data may be missing or out of date. Kindred is not an emergency service.
